The problem

The opportunities exist. What is missing is time to find them and write them well.

You never hear about them

Tenders and calls are published and closed without anyone in the company having seen them.

There is no time

The proposal gets written at the last minute by someone who has another job, between projects.

It does not score

Contracts are lost by a few points, or the bid is excluded for not reaching the technical threshold.

What we do

Three services. You choose which ones.

They are independent. You can start with monitoring only, or only with the writing of one specific proposal.

1 · Watch

We alert you to what fits

Public tenders, calls and open RFPs, filtered by what your company does. Only what deserves a look reaches you.

2 · 72 h analysis

We tell you if it is worth it

What scores, what is required, what must be proven and what your chances are. With that you decide whether to bid.

3 · Writing

We write the proposal

Technical submission for public tenders, client proposal for private bids. Built on your materials, aligned with what scores, ready to deliver.

Where the opportunities come from

Public sources anyone can see. The value is in the filter.

Public tenders

Spain's public procurement platform, with alerts by activity code: IT services, software, consulting. Regional and local portals, and Portugal when relevant.

RFPs and private bids

Supplier portals of large companies and published private tenders. Innovation calls and grants that require a technical proposal. Supplier registration on the portals of the companies you care about, so they invite you.

In the private sector not everything is visible: most RFPs arrive by direct invitation and are never published. That is why portal registration and your own network are part of the service. To fine tune the filter, a presentation or a document about what you do is enough.

Your rules

The business is yours. So is the proposal.

  • Nothing is submitted without your approval. You decide what, when and at what price.
  • Non disclosure agreement signed before we see anything of yours.
  • Your materials are yours. We never reuse one client's content with another, and we delete it when each bid closes.
  • Never two bids for the same tender. And never a tender where we have a conflict of interest.
